Make your pitch clearer

Pitch Deck Reviewer helps you improve the story, evidence, and clarity of your pitch. Upload your deck in ChatGPT or Codex, ask for a slide-by-slide review, or focus on one slide.

The plugin uses review principles curated by Ben Yoskovitz. It examines customer pain, product clarity, market assumptions, traction, pricing, differentiation, go-to-market, team, and the funding ask. It highlights what works, what is unclear, and what to change first.

Try asking:

  • Can you review my pitch deck and give feedback slide by slide?
  • This is my problem slide. What’s missing?
  • Does my storyline make sense and how can I improve it?

Reviews are AI generated. They are not a personal review by Ben and do not guarantee funding. Ben does not charge for using this plugin. Access is subject to the host platform’s availability, account requirements, and any platform charges.
